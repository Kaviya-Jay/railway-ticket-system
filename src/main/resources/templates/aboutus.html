About Us

Welcome to the Sri Lanka Railway Ticket Booking System. Our mission is to provide a seamless, reliable, and efficient platform for all your train travel needs across the island.

This platform was developed to modernize the traditional ticketing process, allowing passengers to check train schedules, view real-time seat availability, and securely book tickets from the comfort of their homes. Whether you are commuting for work or planning a scenic trip to the hill country, our system ensures your journey starts smoothly.

Developed utilizing modern software engineering practices, this system integrates secure user authentication, dynamic seat allocation, and a comprehensive admin panel for efficient transportation management.
